# Pitch skeleton (keywords only)

> Team notes — ideas and decisions are the team's; summaries assisted by AI.

Only keywords and phrases the **team** (or a mentor, when marked) said in today's sessions, with the time of the session. Nothing invented; this is raw material for the team to write the pitch. Order: problem → persona → solution → NASA data → differentiator → validation → future.

## Problem
- Water access varies by region; in the Northeast, drought worsens fires, and the region depends on rain for water and energy (10:24).
- "It rained yesterday" does not help: farmers need what comes next; climate change makes history alone unreliable (11:15).
- "After São João you plant corn", but the date has shifted with climate change, and corn is sensitive to heat (12:05).
- Data is scattered across different sources; centralize it and make it useful (10:36).

## Persona
- Family farming in Brazil, small and medium farmers (10:43, 12:05).
- Uses the phone and WhatsApp a lot, even to sell, but has weak internet (10:43).
- May have trouble reading or writing (12:05).
- Generic "small farmer" apps list these barriers and then ignore them: "the persona was missing" (10:43).

## Solution (direction under discussion, not decided)
- The farmer only says what they will plant; the rest comes from the location (10:43).
- Asynchronous, server-side, no app to install; daily maintenance tips (10:43).
- Conversational bot (Telegram for the MVP) plus a web dashboard for larger farms, on one API (11:15, 12:05).
- Voice in and out (12:05).
- Compare strategies: what the farmer already does plus alternatives, and the farmer picks the priority: water, soil, erosion or income (11:15).

## NASA data
- Vegetation, rain, fires, temperature layers (10:43).
- Many NASA datasets are global: rain, soil moisture, vegetation, climate (12:05).
- Satellite vs ground: validate with local measurements (10:24, 10:19).

## Differentiator
- Persona first, not just another UI (10:43).
- AI only translates results into plain language; the calculations stay deterministic (11:15, 12:05).
- Mentor: the biggest challenge is the farmer's language (12:05).

## Validation
- Mentor: 1–2 farmers or agribusiness people saying "I would use this" (12:05). _Pending._

## Future

- WhatsApp as the channel when resources allow (10:45; decided Nov 14).
- Field device / soil sensor (10:19).
- Mentor: from projection to prescription (12:05).
