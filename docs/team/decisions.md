| Nov 14, 2026 | **Product priority: engine first, then validation, then dashboard.** After a mentor's feedback, and because the judging criteria weigh **validity** and **relevance**, the team builds the **decision engine** first (E1, #44–#47: the farmer's crops, soil, region, season and previous crops in; rotation options with trade-offs, planting schedule and dates per crop, water demand and pest-cycle break out).
